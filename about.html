Founded in 1998, Godawari Industries combines deep agronomic partnerships with world-class dry milling infrastructure to supply India's top commercial and craft breweries.

OUR JOURNEY & PHILOSOPHY

A Quarter Century of
Milling Mastery.

Located in the heart of Maharashtra’s fertile maize belt, Godawari Industries started with a clear conviction: that the secret to superior beer lies not just in the brewing kettle, but in the exactitude of the milling floor.

Over the last 25 years, we have expanded our capacity to over 40,000 Metric Tons annually, installing automated PLC-controlled roller mills, multi-deck plansifters, and optical color sorters. Today, our brewery grits are trusted by master brewers across India and international markets.

OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLES

The Four Pillars of
The Godawari Promise.

Agronomic Integrity

Technological Precision
