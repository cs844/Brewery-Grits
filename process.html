From Kernel to Kettle:
The Science of Milling.

How our proprietary dry-degermination, chilled roller milling, and multi-deck plansifters isolate pure endosperm while stripping away oil and fines.

ENGINEERING INTEGRITY

The Five-Stage
Milling Cycle.

A look inside our automated, PLC-controlled milling facility in Maharashtra.

🌾
Stage 01: Pre-Cleaning & Optical Sorting RAW GRAIN PURIFICATION

Harvested yellow corn passes through rotary drum screens, high-capacity aspirators, and permanent rare-earth magnets to remove chaff, stalks, foreign matter, and tramp metal. High-speed RGB optical color sorters subsequently eject discolored, broken, or insect-damaged kernels with pneumatic air jets.

💧
Stage 02: Intensive Hydro-Conditioning MOISTURE CALIBRATION

Kernels enter automated dampening screws where precise micro-doses of purified water are applied. This selectively hydrates the corn's outer bran skin and germ embryo, rendering them resilient and pliable while keeping the internal starchy endosperm vitreous and brittle for fracture.

⚙️
Stage 03: Dry Impact Degermination OIL REDUCTION < 0.95%

High-speed rotor impactors shatter the conditioned kernels, dislodging the oil-rich germ intact without pulverizing it into the starch stream. Counter-current gravity aspirators separate the lighter germ and pericarp fiber, achieving a remarkable 99.8% degermination purity.

🔬
Stage 04: Chilled Roller Fractional Milling NARROW SIEVE SPECTRUM

The purified endosperm is gradually reduced across multi-pass chilled corrugated rolls running at calibrated differential speeds. Multi-deck square plansifters equipped with precision stainless steel mesh classify the granules into exact US Mesh sizes while purging flour fines under 425 microns.

📦
Stage 05: Cleanroom Packaging & Batch COA Testing TOTAL QUALITY RELEASE

Finished grits pass through in-line metal detectors and final dust aspirators before loading into 25kg/50kg multi-wall bags or 1 MT jumbo totes. Our on-site quality lab verifies fat, moisture, protein, and granulometry before certifying the batch Certificate of Analysis (COA).

LABORATORY EXCELLENCE

Round-The-Clock
Analytical Verification.

Because a brewmaster cannot afford variances between successive grain shipments, our Quality Control team samples every milling shift on calibrated laboratory instruments:

  • ✦ Soxhlet Ether Extraction for lipid/fat content verification (≤ 0.95%)
  • ✦ Ro-Tap Motorized Sieve Analysis for particle size distribution
  • ✦ Karl Fischer & halogen moisture balancing (≤ 13.0%)
  • ✦ Fluorometric & HPLC testing for mycotoxins and aflatoxins (< 5 ppb)
