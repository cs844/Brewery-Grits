100% PURE CORN

Brewery Corn Grits Standard (BG-1)

100% identity-preserved yellow maize (corn). Our premier adjunct for classic cereal cooker mashing. Coarse polygonal geometry ensures rapid wort run-off and zero mash filtration blinding.

  • Particle Sieve (+20 Mesh) 60% – 70%
  • Fat / Oil Content ≤ 0.95%
  • Extract Potential (D.B.) ≥ 82.0%
  • Moisture ≤ 13.0%
